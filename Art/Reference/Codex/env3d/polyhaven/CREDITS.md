# Poly Haven models

Photoscanned lunar rocks from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com), **CC0 1.0** (public domain): `moon_rock_02`, `moon_rock_03`, `moon_rock_04`, `moon_rock_07` (https://polyhaven.com/a/moon_rock_02 etc.; scans by Greg Zaal, Rico Cilliers, Jenelle van Heerden). glTF at 1K. `Tools/Blender/zcom_props.py` (mode "trellis") turns them into the RockBoulder, RockSlab, RockSpire and RockCliff game props.
