# ZCOM — Vertical Slice

Isometric sci-fi extraction shooter prototype (Unity 6000.2.14f1, URP). You drop onto **KEPLER-7**, a small moon
map in the style of an Escape from Duckov level: explore five areas, fight the alien camps that guard them, and loot
supply crates hidden around the map and inside buildings.

## Run it

1. Open this folder in Unity Hub (Unity **6000.2.14f1**).
2. Open `Assets/ZCOM/Scenes/VerticalSlice.unity` and press **Play**.

If the scene is missing or you change a factory script, rebuild it from the menu **ZCOM ▸ Build Vertical Slice Scene**.

## Controls

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| WASD | Move (camera-relative) |
| Mouse | Aim |
| LMB (hold) | Fire equipped weapon |
| R | Reload (auto-reloads when empty) |
| Space / Shift | Dash (brief invulnerability, dodges acid globs) |
| G / RMB (hold → release) | Aim grenade (arc + blast radius), release to throw. 2 charges, recharge 6s |
| Q | Scan pulse: reveals aliens (health bar + distance marker) and unopened crates within 30 m for 7 s, cooldown 12 s |
| E | Interact: open / search supply crates, pick up items on the ground |
| 1 / 2 / 3 or mouse wheel | Switch weapon |
| Tab / I (Esc closes) | Inventory window |
| F (crate open) | Take everything from the crate |
| H | Show / hide controls |
| R / Enter (after death) | Redeploy |

## What's in the slice

- **Gunfeel**: spring-driven weapon recoil, spread bloom (the crosshair shows real spread), tracers, muzzle flash + light,
  ejected brass, physical magazine drop on reload, camera shake + kick, hit-stop on kills, hitmarkers, crits, floating damage numbers.
- **Grenade skill**: ballistic arc preview, bouncing plasma grenade with accelerating beeps, fireball/smoke/sparks/shockwave/scorch,
  knockback, interrupts alien wind-ups, self-damage (25%).
- **Aliens** (all procedurally animated, all wind-ups telegraphed in red):
  - *Skitter*: fast spider swarm, melee lunge.
  - *Spitter*: keeps range and strafes, lobs dodgeable acid.
  - *Brute*: slow tank with a ground-slam AoE (shown as a filling red circle).
- **Inventory (Escape-from-Duckov style)**: a 3-slot weapon loadout (hotkeys 1-3) and a 6x4 backpack grid with stacking
  (Stone 30, Gold 20, Xenite 10 per stack). Icons are rendered from the items' 3D models.
  - LMB drag: move / merge / swap. Shift + drag: split a stack in half. RMB: quick move (backpack <-> loadout, crate -> you).
  - Drag an item outside the window to drop it on the deck; walk up and press E to take it back.
  - Click a weapon in the loadout to equip it. Hover any item for a tooltip with stats.
- **Loot**: aliens sometimes drop resource chunks (Brutes always drop a pile) that get vacuumed in when you walk close.
  Four supply crates are containers: E opens a crate window next to your backpack (drag items over, RMB, or F to take all).
- **Energy weapons**:
  - *Ion Carbine* (starter): full-auto cyan plasma bolts.
  - *Nova Scatter*: 8-shard pink plasma burst, big knockback.
  - *Helix Beam*: hold for a continuous purple laser with twin helix strands that burns through up to 4 aliens and drains its cell.
  Bolts visibly fly and deal damage on impact; weapons have spinning emitter rings, glowing cores and eject their energy cell on recharge.
- **The map (KEPLER-7)**, about 90 x 70 m, ringed by cliffs:
  - *Landing Zone*: your drop pod and a starter crate.
  - *Kepler-7 Outpost*: three enterable buildings (barracks, lab, storage) with interiors and crates; roofs hide when you're inside.
  - *Crash Site*: a burning freighter wreck and a gold crate.
  - *Xeno Nest*: hive spires, eggs and a Brute guarding the Helix Beam.
  - *Mining Ridge*: ore boulders, a drill rig and a xenite crate.
  Each area shows a banner the first time you enter; the top of the HUD shows where you are.
- **Alien camps** instead of waves: aliens idle and wander their area until they see you (or get shot), then the whole
  camp is alerted ("!"). Run far enough and they give up and walk home. Clearing a camp announces it.
- NavMesh pathing around cover and buildings, health-orb drops, HUD, game over (your backpack is lost) + redeploy.
- **Characters modelled in Blender**: the operative, Skitter, Spitter and Brute are generated by a Blender script
  (bevelled hard-surface armour, subdivided organic forms, displaced alien skin), exported as FBX into
  `Assets/ZCOM/Resources/Models/` and split into the parts the game animates (legs, arms, sac). Previews are in `Tools/Blender/previews/`.
- **HUD**: chamfered sci-fi panels and icons generated in code, Rajdhani font (OFL, `Assets/ZCOM/Resources/Fonts`):
  vitals with a segmented health bar, grenade/dash skills, weapon card with icon and cell ticks, loadout tabs,
  location header with threat warning, discovery banners, key-cap prompts. `H` toggles the controls panel.
- Level art, VFX and **audio are procedural** (primitives, generated textures, synthesized SFX).
  Sounds are played by id through `ProcAudio.Play("shot")` etc.

## Raid loop, enemies and outpost (v3)

- **Extraction**: an evac beacon (sky beam, north edge) - stand on the pad for 8 s to extract with your backpack; starting the
  countdown alerts the hive (ambush wave). End-of-raid summary (`Scripts/UI/RaidUI.cs`): raid time, kills, camps, loot
  value secured or lost. **Esc** pauses (R restart, H controls, Q quit).
- **Enemies** (`Tools/Blender/zcom_aliens.py`, designs in `Art/Reference/Codex/env_aliens.png`): Skitter, Spitter and Brute
  built from rigid chitin segments on real armatures; `Scripts/Enemies/InsectLegs.cs` plants every foot in the world and
  steps legs in alternating groups with 2-bone IK, so they walk naturally on the terrain.
- **Outpost**: modular hab kit (`Tools/Blender/zcom_habkit.py`, concept `env_hab.png`) - wall / window panels, ribs, corner
  pillars, airlock door frames with steps, roof tiles, vents, pipes, dishes, beacons, lamp posts and walkway grates;
  `MapBuilder.House` assembles buildings from it (gameplay colliders stay simple boxes).
- **Atmosphere**: moon-wind ambience + a combat tension layer that swells with engaged hostiles (`Fx/AmbientAudio.cs`),
  drifting dust motes (`Fx/AmbientDust.cs`).
- `SmokeTest -zcomTour` now also lines up the three aliens and runs an extraction.

## Map art (v2)

- **Concepts** (Codex): `Art/Reference/Codex/env_wreck.png`, `env_rocks.png`, `env_props.png`, `env_hive.png`, tileable `env_textures.png`
  (made seamless with normal maps into `Art/Reference/Codex/tiles/` and `Assets/ZCOM/Resources/Textures/Terrain/`).
- **Props**: each concept object -> Hunyuan3D-2 sculpt (`Art/Reference/Codex/env3d/*.glb`, not committed: regenerate from the
  `env3d/*.png` crops on the Hugging Face Space `tencent/Hunyuan3D-2`, endpoint `/shape_generation`, 30 steps, seed 1234, octree 320) -> `Tools/Blender/zcom_props.py`
  (spec: `Art/Reference/Codex/props_spec.json`): decimate, bake sculpt normals + AO, albedo from the concept (front) blended
  with the tiling texture, dust/grime, emissive lights -> `Resources/Models/Prop_*.fbx` + `Resources/Textures/Props/`.
  `ZCOM > Rebuild Prop Materials` (also run by the scene builder) makes the URP materials.
- **Terrain**: `MapBuilder.BuildTerrain` sculpts a Unity Terrain (flat play areas, dunes, craters, crash crater, cliffs beyond
  the bounds) and paints regolith / rock (slopes) / dark crater dust / hive creep. Fog + stronger SSAO for depth.
- `SmokeTest -zcomTour` teleports through every area and screenshots it.

## Playable character: Orbital Marshal (v4, TRELLIS.2)

- **Sculpt**: `Art/Reference/Codex/gen3d/front_full.png` (the uncropped front view of the turnaround) -> Hugging Face
  Space `microsoft/TRELLIS.2` (MIT weights; seed 1234, resolution 1024, GLB decimation 300k, texture 2048) ->
  `Art/Reference/Codex/gen3d/marshal_trellis.glb`. Crisper armour than the Hunyuan3D-2 sculpt, and its own texture.
- **Build**: `Tools/Blender/zcom_marine_trellis.py` runs the v3 pipeline below with three changes: the GLB is turned
  to face -Y and welded, the albedo is baked from TRELLIS's texture (brightened; visor / chest lights and rank
  chevrons still projected from the turnaround), and the skinning keeps the cape and torso off the arms.
  Run: `Blender -b --factory-startup -P Tools/Blender/zcom_marine_trellis.py -- Assets/ZCOM/Resources/Models Tools/Blender/previews Art/Reference/Codex/gen3d/marshal_trellis.glb Art/Reference/Codex/marshal_turnaround.png`
- **Library animation**: `Tools/Blender/zcom_marine_mocap.py ... <UAL1_Standard.glb> trellis` retargets Quaternius'
  Universal Animation Library (CC0, not in the repo) onto the same rig -> `MarshalMoves.fbx`: Death, and one jog cycle
  for every locomotion direction (Run; RunBack played backwards; StrafeL/R with the legs turned into the strafe and
  the chest on the aim), so the 2D blend shares one rhythm and phase; Idle keeps the rifle stance and takes its
  breathing, weight shift and head drift from the library idle. The animator cross-fades an Idle state and a Move
  state (the four run directions) on `Speed`.
- **Rifle hold**: chest height with the stock at the shoulder and the left hand on the handguard
  (`GRIP` / `SUPPORT` / `STANCE_YAW` in `zcom_marine_trellis.py`, checked against the real `Weapon_*.fbx`).

- **Codex art pipeline**: the prompts in `Art/Reference/Codex/prompts/` are run with `codex exec` using its built-in image generation. They produced:

## Code map (`Assets/ZCOM`)

| Folder | Contents |
|---|---|
| `Scripts/Core` | `GameManager` (raid: kills, game over), `CameraRig` (iso camera, shake), `GameFeel` (hit-stop), `PlayerInputSource`, material/prim/texture helpers |
| `Scripts/Player` | `PlayerController` (movement, dash, procedural rig), `PlayerHealth`, `Inventory`, `PlayerInteractor` + `IInteractable`, `PlayerFactory` (builds the operative) |
| `Scripts/Items` | `ItemDefs` (item registry, stacks), `ItemContainer` (slots, merge/swap/split), `ItemVisuals`, `ItemIcons` (renders icons) |
| `Scripts/Weapons` | `WeaponDefs` (all weapon stats), `WeaponModels` (sci-fi models), `Gun` (bolts + beam), `GrenadeThrower`, `Grenade` |
| `Scripts/Enemies` | `Alien` (AI + animation, idle/aggro/leash), `AlienCamp` (groups guarding an area), `AlienFactory` (per-type stats & looks), `AlienProjectile` |
| `Scripts/World` | `MapBuilder` (the whole map layout), `MapZone` (named areas), `Building` (roof cutaway), `Smolder` (fires), `LootChest` (container), `ItemPickup` + `LootTable` (drops, crate contents), `Starfield`, `HealthOrb` |
| `Scripts/FX` | `Vfx` (pooled particles/tracers/decals/debris), `ProcAudio` (synth SFX) |
| `Scripts/UI` | `UiKit` (font, palette, generated UI sprites), `Hud`, `InventoryUI` (Duckov-style window, drag & drop) |
| `Editor` | `SliceBuilder` (generates the scene), `SmokeTest` (autopilot play test), `ModelImportSettings` (FBX import) |
| `Tools/Blender` | `zcom_characters.py`: generates the character FBX models + preview renders |

**Tuning**: weapon stats are in `WeaponDefs.cs`, drop rates and crate contents in `LootTable` (`ItemPickup.cs`), stack sizes in `ItemDefs.cs`; grenade/movement values are on the `Player` components in the scene Inspector. Alien stats live in
`AlienFactory.cs`, camps (which aliens, where) and the whole level layout are in `MapBuilder.cs`
(rebuild the scene after editing it).

## Dev: regenerate the character models

Edit `Tools/Blender/zcom_characters.py`, then run (Blender 4.2+):

```bash
/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender -b --factory-startup -P Tools/Blender/zcom_characters.py -- Assets/ZCOM/Resources/Models Tools/Blender/previews
```

Then rebuild the scene in Unity (**ZCOM ▸ Build Vertical Slice Scene**). Object names (`Leg_*`, `Arm_*`, `Sac`, `Mouth`) and
material names (`Armor`, `Suit`, `Skin`, `Shell`, `Glow`, ...) are what the game hooks into.

## Dev: automated smoke test

Runs Play Mode headless with an autopilot that explores the map area by area (pathfinding, fighting camps, looting crates,
using the inventory), saves screenshots plus a top-down `map_overview.png`, and fails on any runtime error:

```bash
/Applications/Unity/Hub/Editor/6000.2.14f1/Unity.app/Contents/MacOS/Unity -batchmode -projectPath . -executeMethod Zcom.EditorTools.SmokeTest.Run -zcomOut Logs/ZcomSmoke -logFile -
```

Add `-zcomPassive` to test the death → restart flow, or `-zcomShowcase` to fire each weapon into open space and screenshot it. It's also available in-editor via **ZCOM ▸ Dev ▸ Run Smoke Test**.
